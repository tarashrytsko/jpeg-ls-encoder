## Consequences for the V5 manuscript

* **CR claim.** V4 states CR ≈ 4.3–7:1 "comfortably above CR ≥ 2.5". That holds only for the Blue Marble composite. On real aerial and Sentinel-2 L1C data the reference CharLS itself reaches 1.20–2.04:1 (Aerials, 8-bit) and 7.0–8.5 bits/pixel (Sentinel-2, i.e. CR 1.52–1.72:1 vs. a 12-bit and 1.88–2.30:1 vs. a 16-bit container). JPEG 2000 and JPEG-XL lossless land within a few percent, so this is the content, not the codec or the partitioning. The Sich-3O CR ≥ 2.5 requirement is therefore **not met by lossless JPEG-LS on sensor-like data**; V5 should say so and point to near-lossless (NEAR ≥ 1) or downlink-scheduling as the remedy.
* **Partitioning penalty** is small on every texture/bit depth (see Req 9 tables), confirming the V4 conclusion that lane count, not direction, matters and costs little.
* **Bit depth.** 12- and 16-bit builds close timing at the mission clock (45 MHz, 12 lanes) — i.e. the same 540 Mpixel/s, now 6.48 / 8.64 Gbit/s of input — at roughly twice the LUTs of the 8-bit build (wider arithmetic, 32-bit output words, 320-bit byte buffer); see the build table under Req 5.
* **Power.** Replace Table VII by the mission rows above; quote the SAIF figure as the activity-based estimate and the vectorless figure as the default-activity estimate.
* **Dependability.** The per-lane partition bounds the loss from any single upset to 1/N of the frame (measured); masking factors per structure are given above; the orbital upset rate is essential bits × λ.
